About this dashboard

  • Computed live from M00124.mdb each time you press Refresh — opened read-only, safe to run while SARAL is in use. Goods billed & bills from the SA sale book; commission from the CM ledger; outstanding = each sale bill minus payments recorded against it in the PY book, aged by bill date against today.
  • Standing risks: the data file is large and approaching Access’s 2 GB ceiling; back it up regularly; nearly all throughput is booked under one person. These don’t change between refreshes.
